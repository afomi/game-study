In art, a study is a copy you make of a master's work to learn how it was made. These are studies of classic games. Each one introduces the game and the world it came from, then takes its key mechanics apart one at a time — screenshots, the variables in memory, the code behind them, and a small working model that reproduces what the original does.

It's made for the fun of it. It's for anyone curious about how these games were built. And it honors the people who made them.
