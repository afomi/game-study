SNK's Baseball Stars came out in Japan on May 19, 1989, and in North America that July.
It was the first baseball game on any console that could save your league to a battery in the cartridge, and the first NES sports game that let you create your own players ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baseball_Stars)).
Twenty-two years later, the producer of EA's NHL games said "Our GM mode is based on Baseball Stars" ([Kotaku, 2011](http://web.archive.org/web/20110925025432/http://www.kotaku.com.au/2011/09/old-school-nes-baseball-game-inspired-ea-sports-world-class-hockey-title/)).

It's Study No. 1, and the game this series was built around.
Its pitcher–batter duel, team building, and league play are the target of a faithful web remake.
Each sketch measures one system in the original and replaces a guess in the remake with a number.
